I think this exhibition deserves more than a portfolio website—it should feel like a **digital extension of the exhibition itself**. After reading your presentation, *2025: A Year of Closing Cycles*, I noticed the narrative is deeply personal, cinematic, and symbolic. Rather than visitors simply scrolling through images, they should experience the emotional journey from beginning to end. 

# Creative Direction

**Theme**

> *A digital gallery exploring endings, transformation, and renewal.*

The experience should feel like walking through a contemporary art museum rather than browsing a website.

Design language:

* Deep charcoal (#111111)
* Warm white (#F5F4F2)
* Soft violet accents (to match your signature 💜)
* Dark burgundy for emotional highlights
* Lots of negative space
* Slow elegant animations
* Large typography
* Minimal interface

Think of brands like:

* Apple
* Aesop
* COS
* Framer Showcase
* Tate Modern digital exhibitions

---

# Technical Stack

I would build it using:

**Frontend**

* Next.js
* React
* TypeScript

**Styling**

* Tailwind CSS
* Framer Motion

**Animations**

* GSAP
* Lenis Smooth Scroll

**3D (optional)**

* Three.js
* React Three Fiber

**CMS**

* Sanity
  or

This lets you easily edit artworks without touching code.
